04 · Questions developers ask

Frequently asked questions

What does a real estate marketing consultant do for a developer?

A consultant looks across the project story, campaigns, lead handling and sales process to find what is limiting enquiries, site visits or bookings. LS Brandworks starts with the available market and funnel evidence, then recommends the positioning, process and channel changes that matter most.

How is a project marketing audit different from running more ads?

More ads increase traffic to the current sales process. Our 15-day project marketing audit checks positioning, campaign performance, lead quality, follow-up and inventory priorities first. It ends with a 90-day action plan; the audit itself does not include execution.

How can a developer improve real estate lead conversion?

Start by measuring each step from enquiry to site visit and booking. Then review lead response, qualification, follow-up and the reasons buyers drop out. Our 45-day Sales Acceleration Program helps teams put changes to positioning, campaigns, sales processes and reporting into practice.

When should marketing for a new real estate project launch be planned?

Before committing the main campaign budget. The buyer profile, competing projects, positioning, lead forecast, media plan and sales channels need to inform one another. Our 90-day New Project Launch engagement builds that plan around the project's market and sales goals.

Do we need to replace our CRM to improve follow-up?

Not necessarily. First check whether enquiries are captured, assigned, followed up and measured consistently in your current setup. CRM automation support can focus on those workflows and reporting gaps before any decision to change software.
